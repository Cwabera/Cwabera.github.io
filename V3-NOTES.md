# V3 — Editorial / Hero Polish

This is a drop-in update for the current Charles Wabera portfolio.

Key fixes:
- The desktop command-center sidebar is preserved as the structural master.
- The hero is split into a dedicated portrait/logo zone and a dedicated title zone.
- The Charles Wabera headline is smaller and width-bounded.
- The portrait uses `object-fit: contain` and is never squeezed.
- The hero logo cannot be injected into the title container.
- Typography, spacing, borders and active navigation are refined toward the Kelvin-style executive command-center finish.
- Existing assets and all other pages are intentionally preserved when this ZIP is extracted over the repository.
